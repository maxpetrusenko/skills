# Voice

## Core Rules

- First line must carry the main value, tension, or insight.
- Make the product legible in one line.
- Show competence through specifics, not self-congratulation.
- Include 1-2 technical nouns for readers who know the topic.
- Sound like a builder who shipped.
- Imply follow or reply interest; never ask for it directly.

## Avoid

- "super excited"
- "game-changing"
- "revolutionary"
- generic hype
- long feature lists
- obvious growth-bait CTAs

## Quality Bar

A good post should:

- communicate user value immediately
- contain one real technical signal
- feel like a shipped artifact, not marketing copy
- make the URL feel like the natural next click

## Failure Modes

- Too much jargon: keep only the nouns that signal real depth.
- Too much product copy: add one technical proof point.
- Too much engineering detail: restate user outcome earlier.
- Weak opening line: rewrite the first sentence before changing anything else.
- Generic CTA: replace with demo or repo link.
