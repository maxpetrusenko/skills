# Examples

Use these as style anchors, not templates to copy.

## Observed Traits

- Opens with a strong claim or compressed workflow.
- Leads with user outcome, not company bio.
- Adds a technical signal for builders who understand the stack.
- Uses a demo or product URL as the natural CTA.
- Implies competence; never asks for attention directly.

## Style Rules To Preserve

- Prefer 2 to 4 short blocks over one wall of text.
- Use arrows sparingly when they compress a workflow well.
- If using hashtags, keep them niche and low count.
- Favor lines that imply "I built something real" over "I am smart."
- Use technical stack names only when they add signal.
